PROJECT 01 // QUANTITATIVE TERMINAL

Eudaimonia — Market Intelligence & Options Terminal

A professional, keyboard-first terminal for Indian financial markets (NSE & BSE). Engineered in Python with Textual, Rich, and SciPy. Features real-time Black-Scholes Greeks, strike-by-strike options chain ladders, Put-Call Ratios, 5-depth order books, and OHLCV candlestick aggregation.
